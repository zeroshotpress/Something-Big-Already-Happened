# Chapter 6: "The Human Cost"

**Subtitle:** 276,000 Layoffs, Trillion-Dollar Selloffs, and the Workers Left Behind

**Word count target:** 3,000 words

---

## Opening Hook

In late 2025, Salesforce cut 4,000 support staff and replaced them with AI agents. Amazon had already cut 14,000 positions. Across the tech industry, 276,000 workers lost their jobs in 2024-2025, with companies increasingly citing AI automation as the reason. By February 2026, 40% of employees reported anxiety about AI replacing their jobs -- up from 28% just two years earlier. These were not hypothetical scenarios from a think-tank report. They were real people, clearing real desks, in an economy that had not yet figured out what comes next.

---

## Key Arguments and Supporting Data

### 1. The layoff numbers
- 2024: 152,000+ tech workers laid off, with companies increasingly citing AI automation
- 2025: 55,000 job cuts directly attributed to AI, out of 1.17 million total layoffs (the highest since the 2020 pandemic)
- Combined 2024-2025: 276,000+ tech workers laid off
- Notable cuts: Amazon (14,000), Salesforce (4,000 support), plus widespread cuts across SaaS, fintech, and consulting
- Microsoft Research identified 5 million white-collar jobs facing extinction -- management analysts, customer service, sales, engineers

### 2. The market shock
- Software stocks began sliding February 4, 2026, amid fears AI would disrupt SaaS business models
- February 5: Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.3-Codex released on the same day
- February 6: Trillion-dollar selloff across software and tech stocks
- The selloff reflected a sudden market consensus: if AI can do 79% of software engineering tasks autonomously for nearly 5 hours at a time, the entire SaaS cost structure is vulnerable
- NVIDIA, by contrast, forecast $65B quarterly revenue -- the infrastructure winners were clear even as the application-layer companies suffered

### 3. The prediction coming true: Amodei's warning
- Dario Amodei (May 2025): "AI will eliminate 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs in 1-5 years"
- Confidence: 75%, Realization: 20% as of Feb 2026
- The realization is only 20% because the full 50% has not yet occurred -- but the trajectory is clear
- Unemployment rose to 4.4% (elevated from recent lows)
- The gap between prediction and realization represents the lag between capability and deployment

### 4. Worker anxiety and the perception gap
- Worker concern about AI job loss: 28% (2024) -> 40% (Feb 2026) per Mercer Global Talent Trends
- But the perception gap worked both ways: many workers underestimated the risk while companies overestimated the savings
- HBR analysis: "Companies are laying off workers because of AI's potential, not its performance"
- Many layoffs were preemptive -- companies cutting headcount based on expected AI capability rather than demonstrated replacement
- This created a cruel dynamic: workers displaced before AI could actually do their jobs, and before retraining programs existed

### 5. The policy landscape: Divergent responses
- **EU**: AI Act entered force Aug 2024; prohibited practices enforced Feb 2025; GPAI obligations from Aug 2025; full high-risk system compliance by Aug 2027 -- the most comprehensive regulatory framework globally
- **US under Trump**: Revoked Biden's AI Executive Order (Jan 2025); shifted from oversight to deregulation; Stargate $500B infrastructure commitment prioritized economic competitiveness
- **State-level**: California's AI law led to disputes (OpenAI allegedly violated it with GPT-5.3-Codex release, Feb 2026)
- The transatlantic divergence created a fragmented landscape: EU workers had more protections; US workers had more exposure but potentially more opportunity
- No major economy had a comprehensive AI transition plan for displaced workers

## Closing Transition to Chapter 7

The layoffs, the market crash, the rising anxiety -- all of it was driven by a technology that was not just improving but beginning to improve itself. The most consequential prediction in the entire scorecard was not about jobs or benchmarks. It was about recursion: AI systems becoming good enough to build the next generation of AI systems. On February 5, 2026, that prediction was confirmed.
